Charity Run

📅 May 10, 2025

⏰ Time: 9:00 AM – 12:00 PM

📍 Location: Newtownards

🎟️ Admission: FREE (Donations encouraged!)

Lace up your sneakers for a great cause! The Newtownards Charity Run is a day of movement, community, and giving back. Whether you’re a seasoned runner or just looking for a fun way to contribute, this event welcomes all ages and fitness levels.

Join us for a day filled with activities, including:

  • 🏅 Choose your distance: Casual fun run or competitive timed race

  • 🎉 Post-run celebrations with live music, food trucks, and awards

  • 💛 All proceeds support help children in need—run with purpose!

  • 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family-friendly atmosphere with activities for kids

Why join?

  • Boost your health while helping others.

  • Celebrate achievements with a supportive community.

  • Every step (or donation) makes a difference!

Find us here in Newtownards!
