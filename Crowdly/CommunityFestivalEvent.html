Community Festival

📅 April 15, 2025

⏰ Time: 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM

📍 Location: Belfast

🎟️ Admission: FREE for all!

Join us for a vibrant day of fun, connection, and celebration at the Belfast Community Festival! This family-friendly event promises something for everyone, featuring:

  • 🎶 Live music performances from local artists

  • 🍔 Delicious food stalls showcasing local cuisine

  • 🎨 Art and craft booths from talented local artisans

  • 🏆 Fun games and activities for all ages

  • 🌳 Community workshops and discussions on sustainability

Whether you’re looking to unwind with friends, entertain the kids, or explore Belfast’s creative spirit, this festival is the perfect way to spend your day. Don’t miss out—mark your calendars and bring the whole family!
