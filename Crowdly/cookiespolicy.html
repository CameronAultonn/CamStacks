What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device to help the website function efficiently and provide a better browsing experience. They allow us to recognize your device and store some information about your preferences or past actions.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic site functionality such as navigation, login, and security.
  • Performance Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our site (e.g., which pages are most visited).
  • Functionality Cookies: Allow the site to remember your preferences such as region or contrast mode settings.
  • Analytics Cookies: Used to collect information about user behavior on our platform to improve our services.

Third-Party Cookies

Some third-party services used on our site, such as social media plugins or embedded maps, may also use cookies. These cookies are managed by the respective third parties and not controlled by Crowdly.

Managing Cookies

You can choose to accept or refuse cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can usually modify your settings to decline them if you prefer.

Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality and features of our website.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology or legal requirements. All changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
