Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 8, 2025

Last Updated: April 8, 2025

At Crowdly, your privacy matters. Whether you’re exploring events, submitting your own, or sharing your story, we’re committed to protecting your information.

1. Who We Are

Crowdly helps communities connect through:

  • Local and virtual events
  • User-submitted event requests
  • Inspiring community stories
  • A live event calendar
  • An interactive map for event locations

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information:

  • Name and email address
  • User-submitted content
  • Location (if shared)

Usage Data:

  • IP address
  • Device/browser details
  • Interaction with calendar & pages

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Display events and stories
  • Show events near you using maps
  • Improve platform functionality

4. Story Submissions & Event Requests

You own what you share. By submitting, you allow us to display content on the platform—with your permission. You may remain anonymous or choose a display name.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your data. We only share info with:

  • Trusted mapping and calendar services
  • Legal authorities when necessary

6. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies to personalize your experience. You can disable cookies via your browser settings.

7. Participation of Children

Crowdly welcomes attendees of all ages, provided a parent or guardian is present. Specific age restrictions may apply for certain events (like daycare), and will be clearly stated in the event details.

We do not knowingly collect data from children without guardian consent.

8. Your Rights

  • Access or modify your data
  • Request content removal
  • Withdraw consent for published content

Contact us at privacy@crowdly.com

9. Security

Your data is protected using modern encryption and security measures. We value your trust.

10. Policy Updates

This policy may change. The latest version will always be posted here with an updated date.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our privacy policy, feel free to contact us
