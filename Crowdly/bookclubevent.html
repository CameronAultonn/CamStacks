Community Book Club

📅 May 31, 2025

⏰ Time: 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM

📍 Location: Belfast

🎟️ Admission: FREE

Join us for an exciting escape into the captivating worlds of books! Let your imagination soar as we explore new stories, characters, and adventures. Dive into a universe where anything is possible—come be part of the journey!

Join us for a day of literary exploration and community-building in our book club, where you can share your thoughts and hear from others. Activities include:

  • 📚 Group discussions of exciting books across various genres

  • 🎉 Social events to meet fellow book enthusiasts and share recommendations

  • 💛 Every meeting fosters a love of reading and learning within the community

  • 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family-friendly sessions with book recommendations for all ages

Why join?

  • Expand your knowledge while enjoying great books with friends.

  • Meet new people and be part of a supportive, engaging group.

  • Every book you read opens up new ideas and discussions!

Find us here in Newtownard!
